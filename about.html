🌐 Welcome to my Journey - A Development and Humanitarian Professional 🚀

Professionally, I am a Hydrogeologist with experience in the Water, Sanitation and Hygiene (WASH) Sector. I am passionate about development and humanitarian aid, data analytics and management. Read here for insights!.

👨‍💻 Discover Me Here

I am a certified Project Management for Development Professional (Project DPro), and MEAL Development Professional (MEAL DPro), with over 10 years of progressive experience in international humanitarian and development programming across Nigeria. I am currently serving as Country Deputy Head of Programs at Jesuit Refugee Service (JRS) Nigeria, where I provide strategic leadership and technical oversight across a multi-donor, multi-sector programme portfolio spanning Northeast, Northwest, Northcentral, and South-South Nigeria.

My career has been shaped by a deep commitment to evidence-based programming, data integrity, and accountable service delivery for the most vulnerable including internally displaced persons (IDPs), returnees, refugees, asylum seekers, trafficked populations, orphans and vulnerable children (OVCs), and host communities affected by conflict, insecurity, and humanitarian crises.

👨‍💻 Program and Project Leadership

Across my career, I have led the design, implementation, coordination, and evaluation of large-scale, multi-partner, donor-funded projects covering Education in Emergencies, Water Sanitation and Hygiene (WASH), Livelihoods and Climate-Smart Agriculture, Protection (including GBV, CRSV, Legal Aid, and MHPSS), Gender and Social Inclusion, Peace-Building and Reconciliation, and Women's Economic Empowerment. I have managed donor relationships and delivered programmes funded by the EU/ECHO, UNHCR, USAID/PEPFAR, GIZ, the Basque Government, Caritas Canada, UNICEF, UNFPA, the World Bank, and several others. I am experienced in developing and managing costed workplans, results frameworks, risk registers, and grant management processes, coordinating effectively across field offices, implementing partners, government agencies, and international stakeholders to deliver measurable impact on the ground.

👨‍💻 MEAL & Research Expertise

Monitoring, Evaluation, Accountability, Research, and Learning (MEARL) is at the core of my professional identity. I have designed and operationalised comprehensive MEAL systems including logframes, theories of change, indicator performance tracking tools (IPTTs), M&E plans, and real-time reporting dashboards aligned with the standards of diverse donors and global humanitarian frameworks. I have led and managed baseline, midline, and endline evaluations; designed and conducted Data Quality Assessments (DQAs) and Programme Quality Assurance (PQA) exercises; and pioneered community-based operations research to generate evidence on social and cultural determinants of health and vulnerability. I am proficient in both qualitative and quantitative research methodologies, with hands-on expertise in KoboToolbox, CSPro, EpiData, EpiInfo, SPSS, NVivo, ArcGIS, and Microsoft Power BI including building real-time dashboards integrated via API for live programme monitoring.

👨‍💻 WASH Technical Specialisation

I hold a Master's degree in Hydrogeology and bring specialist technical expertise to WASH programming. I have successfully led WASH interventions — including the design and construction of solar-powered borehole schemes, hand-pump boreholes, gender-segregated sanitation facilities, and handwashing infrastructure, improving access to safe water and sanitation for over 20,000 school and community beneficiaries across Borno, Adamawa, Taraba, Zamfara, and Abuja. I have conducted comprehensive water safety, quality, and geophysical surveys, and have established and monitored WASH Committees (WASHCOMs) as sustainability and governance mechanisms across project communities.

👨‍💻 Data Management & Technology

I bring advanced data management skills to every role I occupy. I am proficient in mobile data collection platforms (KoboCollect, CommCare, CSEntry, ODK), database programming (EpiData, CSPro), geospatial analysis (ArcGIS), and data visualisation. I have trained and supervised enumerators, research assistants, and M&E Officers across multiple states, and have designed digital data collection systems deployed on Android devices for large-scale field surveys and GIS mapping exercises.

👨‍💻 Partnerships & Capacity Strengthening

I have provided M&E and programme management support to over 41 NGOs, CBOs, and FBOs, and have built the technical capacity of government partners, CSO staff, community volunteers, and data clerks across Akwa Ibom, Cross River, Borno, Adamawa, Taraba, and Zamfara States. I have served on state-level technical working groups including the Cross River State M&E Technical Working Group, Domestic Resource Mobilisation Technical Working Group, and Family Planning Advocacy Working Group contributing to policy development and government system strengthening.

👨‍💻 Research & Publications

I have contributed to peer-reviewed research, including a published study on the roles of community-based health workers in Nigeria's HIV/AIDS epidemic control targets (IJREHC, 2024), and a co-authored assessment of groundwater quality in Akwa Ibom State (Global Journal of Geological Sciences, 2024) and co-authored assessment on the Impact of Oil Contamination in Okoro Utip and Eteo Communities, Rivers and Akwa Ibom States.

👨‍💻 More...

I am currently pursuing an International Diploma in Humanitarian Assistance (IDHA) at Fordham University, Pretoria - South Africa and a Doctor of Philosophy (PhD) in Migration Studies at Nnamdi Azikiwe University, Nigeria, building on my practice-based expertise in displacement, forced migration, and humanitarian response.

I am driven by a firm belief that quality data, rigorous evidence, and adaptive learning are essential to delivering programmes that truly transform lives and I bring that conviction to every project, partnership, and community I serve.
